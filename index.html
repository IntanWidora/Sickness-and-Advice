How to Give Advice with Should & Shouldn't

When someone is sick, we can give them advice (nasihat) using modal verbs "should" (harus/sebaiknya) or "shouldn't" / "should not" (sebaiknya tidak).

Positive Advice (You Should...)
Subject + should + Verb 1
  • You should rest in bed.
  • He should see a dentist.
  • She should drink warm water.
Negative Advice (You Shouldn't...)
Subject + shouldn't + Verb 1
  • You shouldn't drink ice water.
  • He shouldn't eat spicy food.
  • She shouldn't play outside.

Sample Dialogue at the Doctor's Clinic

🧑‍🎓

Patient (Budi):

"Doctor, I have a terrible stomachache. What should I do?"

👨‍⚕️
